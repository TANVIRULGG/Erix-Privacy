# Erix Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Erix ("the bot", "we") is a Discord music bot. This policy explains what data the bot uses and how to ask us to delete it. Contact details are at the bottom.

## 1. What we collect and why

| Data | Why | Stored? |
|---|---|---|
| Server (guild) ID and settings: prefix, welcome message setup, request channel, DJ role, 24/7 mode, premium status | To remember each server's configuration | Yes |
| User ID | For your playlists, favourites, listening history, AFK status, custom prefix, vote and premium status | Yes |
| Playlists, favourites, recently played tracks (track titles/IDs) | Music features you use | Yes |
| AFK reason and mentions received while AFK (who mentioned you and a message link) | AFK feature | Yes, cleared when you return from AFK |
| Voice channel IDs, join and leave events | To play music and manage temporary voice channels | Only as needed to run the feature |

## 2. Message content
Erix reads message text in servers to detect commands (for example a prefix command like `!play`) and to handle the song request channel. **Message content is processed in memory only. We do not store, log, sell, analyse, or share message content, and we never use it to train machine learning or AI models.** Messages that are not commands or song requests are ignored.

## 3. Server member events
The bot receives member join events only to send the welcome message a server administrator configured. We do not store member profiles, and we do not collect presence, status, or activity data.

## 4. What we do not do
- We do not sell or share data with advertisers, data brokers, or third parties.
- We do not profile users or track them outside Discord.
- We do not collect passwords, tokens, or payment details.
- We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

## 5. Where data is stored and how it is protected
Data is stored in a MongoDB database. We restrict access to the database and protect it with access credentials. Stored data is encrypted at rest. We do not keep data longer than needed for the features above.

## 6. Retention and deletion
- Server settings are removed when the bot is removed from a server and you ask us to delete them.
- Playlists, favourites, history, and AFK data stay until you delete them with the bot's commands or ask us to delete them.
- To request deletion of your data, contact us using the details below. We delete it within 30 days.

## 7. Third-party services
Music is played through a Lavalink audio server. Track searches (title or link) are sent to music source providers (such as YouTube or Spotify) to find audio. Only the search text or link is sent, not your Discord identity.

## 8. Changes
We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
